Predictions (Rayleigh perturbation theory)

Δf/f = −(Δm · |ψ(x,y)|²) / (2 · m_eff) where ψ is mode shape amplitude at putty position.

Plate mass = 1.375g, 4× PZT mass ≈ 1.2g → m_eff ≈ 2.6g (effective).
Putty Δm = 0.050g → Δm/m_eff = 1.9%
At pure antinode (|ψ|²=1): Δf/f ≈ −0.96%
