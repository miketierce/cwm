### 2. Clipping qualification before freezing anything (FT-M0)
**Full 181-point grid (30–120 kHz, 500 Hz steps)**, CH1 and CH2 at 10% duty, scope on the ±10V range, raw waveform peaks:

| | Ch A waveform peak, max | Points above 4.0V | Points above 3.5V | Ch B waveform peak, max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CH1 | **5.35V** at 58.5 kHz (fundamental only 3.51V) | 8 | 15 | 0.44V |
| CH2 | **6.24V** at 35.0 kHz (fundamental only 2.09V) | 6 | 11 | 3.22V |

**Finding:** at 10% duty the planned ±5V Ch A range would clip about 5–8 grid points per TX. The clip rule would then exclude the strongest modes.

**Cause: harmonics.** A 10%-duty square wave puts nearly as much drive into 2f, 3f, … as into f:

| Harmonic | Relative drive, sin(nπd)/n at d = 0.1 |
|---|---|
| f | 0.31 |
| 2f | 0.29 |
| 3f | 0.27 |

At 35 kHz, the waveform peak is set by the response at 70 and 105 kHz, and 105 kHz is itself a strong mode.

**Consequence for simulations:** every trial drives the plate at several frequencies at once. The capture tool now records the 2f and 3f responses on both channels for every trial, and the raw captures keep every harmonic.

**Decision (resolved: option C, with Ch A on ±5V instead of ±2V; see section 5):**
- **A, recommended:** change the preamp gain to ×11 (Rg 1 kΩ → 4.7 kΩ, keeping the 10 µF) and drive at 50% duty.
  - Even harmonics vanish and 3f falls to 1/3, so the drive is much closer to a pure tone.
  - The ±5V range and its resolution are kept.
  - The fundamental at the output becomes about 0.74× its current level.
  - Repeat the qualification sweep after the change.
- **B:** keep ×48 and 10% duty, with Ch A on the ±10V range.
  - No hardware change, but roughly twice the quantization noise.
  - Even harmonics stay as strong as the fundamental.
  - Peaks reach about 6.2V, against roughly ±7.5V of swing on fresh rails.
- **C, chosen:** change the gain to ×11 (Rg → 4.7 kΩ) but keep 10% duty. Ch A was planned for the ±2V range; measured peaks moved it to ±5V (section 5). Ch B stays on ±5V.
  - Keeps the strong 2f/3f content, which allows a built-in harmonic-prediction test.
  - Expected Ch A peaks about 1.4V.
  - Resolution similar to A.
  - Needs separate scope ranges per channel in the tool, and a repeat qualification sweep.

**Note on harmonics and claims:**
- The 2f/3f responses come from harmonics already present in the square-wave drive, passing through a linear plate. They are not generated by the glass.
- Predicting them from H measured at 2f/3f in separate trials is a test of linear superposition and of the frozen transfer model (FT-M2), and groundwork for FT-3.
- It is not entanglement, and not the classical frequency × space non-separability recorded in `paper/CLAIMS_STATUS.md`.
- Harmonics generated by the glass from a pure tone would be nonlinearity (CLAIMS_STATUS F10, so far not observed).

**Range decision: Ch A ±5V, Ch B ±5V, frozen for the campaign.**
- ±2V would clip at 0.98 × 2V = 1.96V, only about 1.9 dB above the 1.58V peak.
- Steep resonance flanks can move that much with small temperature shifts between sessions.
- Cost: quantization is about 2.5× coarser than ±2V. The expected demod noise floor is about 0.5 mV, so the median signal-to-noise ratio is still about 50 dB.

**Aliasing at ×11:**
- The preamp's bandwidth grew from about 166 kHz to about 730 kHz. Drive harmonics above the 390.6 kHz Nyquist limit now fold back into the band.
- Spot checks, strongest folded line relative to the drive-frequency response:

| Drive | Strongest folded line |
|---|---|
| CH2, 105 kHz | −22 dB at 361 kHz |
| CH1, 58.5 kHz | −30 dB |
| CH2, 35 kHz | −29 dB |
| CH1, 90 kHz | −16 dB at 331 kHz (5f = 450 kHz folded) |

- None of these landed within 1 kHz of f, 2f or 3f.
- Ch B is exposed too: it sees the drive's square edges directly, with the scope's 10 MHz bandwidth.
- **The affected points depend only on the grid and sample rate:** a folded m·f lands on n·f when f ≈ 781,250/(m ± n). They can be listed before any data.

### 6. Preregistered analysis: harmonic superposition (written before any S1 data)
**Question:** does the end-to-end system behave as a linear, time-invariant transfer? The chain is drive terminal → plate → RX → preamp → Ch A. If it does, the harmonic responses seen in one trial are predicted by the transfer measured at those frequencies in separate trials.

**Claim boundary:**
- This tests linearity and superposition, electronics included.
- It is not entanglement and not the classical frequency × space non-separability result.
- A failure would point to nonlinearity somewhere in the chain. It would not, by itself, show that the glass computes.

**Data:**
- GLASS blocks, separately per TX (CH1, CH2) and per session.
- A trial is valid with no overflow or clip.
- The reference at the frequency used must be ≥ 20× the QUIET noise at that grid frequency.

**Transfer estimate:** Ĥ(g) = mean over valid trials at grid point g of zA/zB at the drive frequency.

**Prediction:**
- For each valid trial at drive frequency f and order n ∈ {2, 3} with n·f ≤ 120 kHz: predicted zA_n = Ĥ(n·f) × zB_n, where zB_n is measured in the same trial.
- Harmonic reference validity: |zB_n| ≥ 20× QUIET noise at n·f.
- Eligible drive frequencies: 30–60 kHz for n = 2 (61 grid points) and 30–40 kHz for n = 3 (21 grid points).
- The quantized NCO frequencies n·SYS_CLK/round(SYS_CLK/f) and SYS_CLK/round(SYS_CLK/(n·f)) differ by under 0.3 Hz. That is negligible against mode bandwidths of 100 Hz or more.

**Exclusions, fixed now:** drop a (trial, n) pair if f is alias-flagged for order n, or if n·f is alias-flagged for order 1 (that is, Ĥ(n·f) itself is contaminated). Lists as in section 5.

**Metrics per eligible (f, n) point:**
- Primary: complex normalized RMS error e = sqrt(mean|zA_n − predicted|²) / sqrt(mean|zA_n|²).
- Secondary: magnitude-only error, reported separately; the two observables are never compared with each other (FT-M2).
- Summary per n: median and 90th percentile of e across points.

**Tiers:**
- (i) Within-session: Ĥ and predictions from the same session, for each of S1, S2 and S3.
- (ii) **Held out, primary:** Ĥ frozen from S1 + S2 pooled, predictions on S3 trials with no refitting.

**Outcomes (thresholds fixed now):**
- **SUPPORTED:** within-session median e ≤ 0.05 for both n = 2 and n = 3, **and** held-out S3 median e ≤ 0.10, with ≥ 80% of eligible points valid.
- **REJECTED:** median e > 0.20 in any tier with a consistent bias in magnitude or phase.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else, or fewer than 80% of eligible points valid.

**Context (not decision criteria):**
- The noise-limited error expected from the QUIET floor.
- Exploratory: e at alias-flagged versus unflagged points, to see whether contamination shows up.
