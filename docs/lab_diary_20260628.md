## Workability assessment

- **Channel budget fits exactly (15):** 8 PIO = A(4)+C(4); 7 PWM = D2+E2+F2+G1. Plate B's 3 cascade TX come from the amp board (off-NCO).
- **Plate B 4th TX = CH13/GP10** (a real PWM tone). Collides with the small-plate budget (CH13 was for D–G), so either one small plate drops to 1 TX, or CH13 broadcasts to B + a slide (different bands, OK). **Keep it OFF during cascade runs** — a 3.3 V direct drive swamps the ~1000× weaker cascaded signal; it's only for standalone B census.
- **Confirm:** (1) plate A is NCO-driven (Block A) — only its RX were listed; (2) plate B has a pickup → Ch A; (3) plate C has a pickup → Ch A.
- **Plate A/B must be the matched 100mm pair** (same mass) so A's output lands on B's resonances; plate C is the independent odd one.

## Bring-up debugged: dead op-amp HALF on Ch A preamp; cascade confirmed (17:xx)

Verify chase: large+small plates all read ~2× regardless of gain (×11/×48/1600) AND regardless of the 4.7k RX summing. Decisive clue — preamp **power off vs on = identical Ch A** → preamp not in path. Swapped the Ch A preamp with a cascade-board channel (RX1/B-TX1): **massive SNR jump.** The OPA2134 had a **dead half** — TX half drove fine, RX half didn't (off=on). Lesson: bench each op-amp half before trusting a rebuild.

Post-swap verify (×48 readout + 4.7k summing): **all 15 channels OK, RX 24–32×** (was ~2×). F1–4 (Plate A→cascade→B) **31–32×** = cascade transfers. F5–8 Plate C 30–32×. D 24–25×, E/F/G 26–30×. Drive 600–1200× all. Phase lock 39–46%. Array verified functional.
