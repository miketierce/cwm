**Predictions** (both sessions, 2f and 3f):

### 6. Exact-harmonic grid pilot: result (12:31–12:45)
**Runs:** `XG-F2-A5`, then `XG-F2-A2`, as preregistered (§5). The diary hash was rechecked before scoring, and the scoring script is unchanged. Each GLASS block has 3,760 trials (188 points × 20) with 0 overflow and 0 clip. Every eligible point is valid (57/57 at 2f, 19/19 at 3f).

| Session | Grid | Ch A range | 2f: median e (p90) | 3f: median e (p90) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| XG-F2-A5 | exact-harmonic | ±5 V | 0.014 (0.044) | **0.028 (0.051)** |
| XG-F2-A2 | exact-harmonic | ±2 V | 0.010 (0.018) | 0.014 (0.020) |
| XR-F2-A5-1, -2 (Oct 27) | FT-1 | ±5 V | 0.034–0.036 (0.23–0.25) | 0.055–0.056 (0.32) |
| XR-F2-A2-1, -2 (Oct 27) | FT-1 | ±2 V | 0.016–0.017 (0.25) | 0.084 (0.31) |

- **Preregistered outcome: partial.** P1 and P2 hold on ±2 V at both orders, and on ±5 V at 2f. On ±5 V at 3f the median (0.028) and p90 (0.051) are just over the bounds (0.025 and 0.05). No median is above 0.05.
- **The mismatched cluster is gone.** Every point now scores like the matched points did, and the 90th percentile fell from 0.23–0.32 on the FT-1 grid to 0.02–0.05. The former boundary point, 33 kHz → 99 kHz, scores 0.036 on ±5 V and 0.006 on ±2 V (FT-1 grid: 0.056 and 0.084).
- **Ch A's quantization limits ±5 V at 3f.** The worst 3f points on ±5 V (targets 108–118 kHz, e 0.05–0.08) fall to 0.010–0.022 on ±2 V. The 3f median halves on ±2 V (ratio 0.49) and the 2f median falls by a third (0.68).
- **For the rerun:** the exact-harmonic grid with Ch A on ±2 V. On this path, within-session superposition then holds to a median of 1.0% at 2f and 1.4% at 3f, with 90% of points under 2%. A within-session threshold of a few percent leaves margin. The held-out tier depends on how still the plate stays (§2; Oct 27 §10).
