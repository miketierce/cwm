**Between sessions, O2** (`tools/k_eff_ft1_drift.py --session FT1-S2 --compare FT1-S1`, new): ln(H_S2 / H_S1) = g + c·P(f) on the per-frequency GLASS means, with the same point selection as within-block.

| Path | Points | Gain change | Phase change | Common mode shift | Variance explained (gain only / + shift) | Median change → residual |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CH1 | 114 | −0.35 ± 0.27% | +0.97 ± 0.16° | −2 ± 21 ppm | 12% / 12% | 2.7% → 2.8% |
| CH2 | 109 | −0.41 ± 0.35% | +0.70 ± 0.19° | −41 ± 27 ppm | 4% / 5% | 3.5% → 3.4% |

- **The S1 → S2 change is not a uniform gain change or a common mode shift.** Together those explain 5–12% of the weighted variance and leave the median change at about 3%.
- **It is proportional to H** (inline check): the relative change is about the same in each third of |H| (2.3–4.2%), while the absolute change grows with |H|. An additive electrical term would do the opposite.
- **It is not the feedthrough** (inline check): subtracting each session's ELECTRICAL_ONLY H makes the change larger. Over all points with RX SNR ≥ 30, CH1 goes from 3.1% to 4.0% and CH2 from 3.5% to 4.1%. The dummy's 1.7× rise belongs to the dummy and doesn't carry over to the GLASS state.
- So individual modes changed by different amounts between sessions. Candidates are transducer bonds or mounting stress after the power-down, or uneven temperature; the data can't separate them yet.
- **No temperature coefficient yet:** the air temperature differed by only 0.2°C (22.7 → 22.9°C), and the shift uncertainty is ±21–27 ppm.
- **For FT-M2:** within a session H repeats to about 0.5% per trial; between sessions it moves about 3% (median; p90 about 10%). That between-session term is the floor for any frozen-model prediction on S3.
- **Rough expectation, written before S3:** a ~3% between-session error added to the 0.04–0.06 within-session errors puts the held-out superposition median around 0.06, under the 0.10 threshold. This is not a decision criterion.
